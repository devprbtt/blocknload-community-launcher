## v2.1.14

### New Features
- **Death Cam HP**: When spectating a teammate during the death cam, their health bar and current HP are now displayed. A visual health bar (using Unicode block characters) and HP numbers (e.g., `Prbtt ████████░░ 150/200`) appear on the death cam UI.

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed team healthbars showing for non-player units (devices, land mines, etc.) during death cam — now only actual player units with a `PlayerId` are shown
- Fixed player nickname display — uses `PlayerData.FindFriend()` to look up the real player nickname instead of showing the Unity GameObject name

### Technical Details
- Added `DeathCamRuntime` helper class in `BnlCommunityFixes.dll` with `IsDeathCamFriendly()` and `UpdateDeathCamHpText()` methods
- Patches `GuiHealthbar.IsUnitAvailableForShow()` and `AlphaUpdate()` to make friendy healthbars visible during death cam
- Patches `GuiDeathCameraTargets.Update()` to append HP info with visual bar to the death cam nickname text
- `IsDeathCamFriendly()` now filters by `Unit.PlayerId.HasValue` to exclude non-player entities
